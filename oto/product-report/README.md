# Report product — what its specification must say

Extends `product` (the core) and `report` (the domain). Thin by decision: a report product's
specification is obligations typed by what they govern — the identity of a document, its data, its
layout, its delivery, compliance, the operation of runs, what must stay answerable afterwards,
readiness — each linked (`governs`) to the report type, field, channel or policy in the catalogue
it is about. The report types, templates, fields, columns and release policies themselves are the
`report` domain; the pipeline that produces them is design.

The ten questions a report product owner asks were confirmed by Chiheb Dkhil on 2026-10-06
(`questions.json`, RP1–RP10). Readiness (RP10) is computed from the domain, never declared.
